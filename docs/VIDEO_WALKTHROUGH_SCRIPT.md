# Lift — Product Walkthrough Video Script

Primary use: landing page hero / anywhere we introduce Lift to a cold prospect.
Audience: Mike, the owner-operator (see `docs/PERSONA.md`). Target length: **~2:30**.
Voice: plain, blunt, mechanic-shop direct (see `docs/EMAIL_CAMPAIGN_BRIEF.md` §4).

---

## Production notes

- **Visual language:** match the "Service Manual" landing aesthetic — newsprint cream, warm ink, Snap-On red accents, Space Mono labels. Screen recordings of the actual PWA on a phone, not desktop. Phone mockups of SMS threads beat stock shop footage every time.
- **Auto-reply bubbles** keep the red 1px border + "Auto-replied · status check" tag — that's the trust convention.
- **Sound design:** open with real shop sounds (impact wrench, air ratchet) cut hard by a phone buzz. That contrast IS the hook.
- **VO casting:** a real, unpolished voice. Someone who sounds like they've held a torque wrench. Not announcer-smooth.
- **No fabricated testimonials, stats, or customer names.** Proof is product mechanics shown on screen.
- **Captions on by default** — most landing-page plays are muted. Every scene must work silently, so on-screen text carries the message too.

---

## Script

### SCENE 1 — Cold open: the interruption (0:00–0:10)

**VISUAL:** Tight shot, hands on a caliper, impact wrench running. A phone buzzes on the toolbox. Buzzes again. Wrench stops. Cut to the phone screen: three stacked texts — "is my car ready?" / "any update?" / "hello?"

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** `HOW MANY TIMES DID THIS HAPPEN TODAY?`

**VO:**
> How many times did a customer text you "is my car ready" today? Three? Eight? Every single one pulled you out from under a hood.

---

### SCENE 2 — What Lift is (0:10–0:22)

**VISUAL:** Hard cut to cream background. LIFT wordmark stamps in. Then a phone in a greasy hand, app open to the board.

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** `§ 01 / RUN THE WHOLE SHOP FROM YOUR PHONE`

**VO:**
> This is Lift. It's a shop app built for 1–3 bay independents — the owner who's also the tech, the front counter, and the bookkeeper. The whole shop runs from the phone in your pocket: dead-simple ROs, invoices, and customer texts. You talk, it service-writes.

---

### SCENE 3 — The wedge: auto-reply (0:22–0:48)

**VISUAL:** Screen recording. Inbound text arrives: "hey is the camry done yet?" Beat. Lift's reply appears — red-bordered bubble, tagged "Auto-replied · status check": actual RO status and pickup ETA. Cut to the same hands, still on the caliper — never touched the phone.

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** `ANSWERED IN UNDER 10 SECONDS. YOU NEVER STOPPED WORKING.`

**VO:**
> Start with the part that steals the most wrench time. A customer texts your shop number. Lift reads it, checks the RO, and answers with the real status — in under ten seconds. You never touched your phone.
>
> It only auto-replies to status checks. A real question, a complaint, anything that needs you — that comes straight to you, untouched. And every auto-reply is tagged, so you can read exactly what it said. There's a kill switch in settings if you ever want it off.

---

### SCENE 4 — Write up a car from your phone (0:48–1:12)

**VISUAL:** New RO flow. Scan the VIN with the camera → year/make/model fill themselves. Tap the mic, talk. Line items appear with prices. Snap two photos of a seized caliper from inside the RO.

**DEMO AUDIO (owner speaking into phone):**
> "Front pads and rotors, left caliper's seized, call it two and a half hours."

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** `YOU TALK, IT SERVICE-WRITES.`

**VO:**
> Writing up a car takes about a minute. Scan the VIN — Lift decodes it and fills in the vehicle. Then just say what you found. Lift turns it into line items at your labor rate. Snap photos right on the RO — the seized caliper, the rotted line — so the evidence lives with the job, not buried in your camera roll.

---

### SCENE 5 — AI-drafted estimates you approve (1:12–1:38)

**VISUAL:** Tap "Send estimate." Draft sheet slides up with a plain-English text, cursor edits one word, thumb hits Send. Cut to customer's phone: clean estimate text with a link → tap → approve page → APPROVED. Back in the app, RO status flips to "In repair" by itself.

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** `YOU APPROVE EVERY WORD BEFORE IT SENDS.`

**VO:**
> Tap send estimate, and Lift drafts the text for you — plain English, not mechanic-speak, no scrap paper. You read it, change anything you want, and hit send. Nothing goes to a customer you didn't approve.
>
> They get a clean estimate with a link. One tap to approve, and the RO moves itself to "in repair." No phone tag. No "what did you say it'd be again?"

---

### SCENE 6 — Get paid before pickup (1:38–1:58)

**VISUAL:** RO moved to "Ready." Outbound text fires: "She's ready" + pay link. Customer pays from their couch. PAID badge stamps onto the RO in red.

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** `PAID BEFORE THE CAR LEAVES THE LOT.`

**VO:**
> When the car's ready, the customer gets a text with a pay link. They pay from their phone before they even show up. Keys change hands, done — no fumbling for a wallet at the counter, no chasing money after the car's gone.

---

### SCENE 7 — The board (1:58–2:10)

**VISUAL:** Pull back to the board — today's cars grouped by status, thumb scrolls, drags one card from "Ready" toward done. Phone slides into a shirt pocket.

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** `YOUR WHOLE DAY. ONE SCREEN. ON YOUR PHONE.`

**VO:**
> Everything lives on one board. Today's cars, grouped by status, on the phone that's already in your pocket. No back-office computer. No Sunday night data entry.

---

### SCENE 8 — Price + CTA (2:10–2:35)

**VISUAL:** Single pricing card, square corners, red CTA. `$79/MO FLAT` in Archivo Black. Below it, mono type: `UNLIMITED TECHS · UNLIMITED ROs · UNLIMITED TEXTS`. Then the CTA button: `START YOUR 14-DAY TRIAL`. Final frame: LIFT wordmark + `lift.worxel.com`.

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** `$79/MO. THAT'S IT. NO PER-TECH GAMES.`

**VO:**
> Seventy-nine bucks a month, flat. Unlimited techs, unlimited ROs, unlimited texts. No per-seat fees, no add-ons, no surprises on the statement.
>
> Setup is three screens — about ten minutes — and you'll send your first AI-drafted estimate the same afternoon. Fourteen-day trial, no card required. And your data's yours: one-click export, anytime, even if you leave.
>
> Lift. Get back in the bay.

## Cutdown guidance

- **:30 cut (paid placements, if ever):** Scenes 1 → 3 → 8. The interruption, the auto-reply, the price.
- **:60 cut:** add Scene 5 (approve every word) — it's the #1 objection killer.
- The full 2:30 is for the landing page and warm links (email CTA "watch the 2-minute walkthrough").

## Voice checklist (before recording)

- [ ] No "platform / solution / seamless / powerful / unlock" anywhere.
- [ ] Specific numbers: $79, 10 seconds, 10 minutes, 14 days, 1–3 bays.
- [ ] "RO" said as R-O, never "repair order" spelled out in VO.
- [ ] Every claim shown on screen is a real product mechanic — nothing staged that the app can't do.
- [ ] Anti-persona untouched: nothing implies multi-bay, service advisors, or fleets.
