# Lift — Walk-In Brochure Copy (v1 draft)

Step 1 of the brochure project: copy only. Design (Service Manual aesthetic) and print specs come next.

**Format assumption:** half-fold or trifold, but written so the FRONT panel works standalone —
the realistic scenario is a 3-second glance before it hits the counter. Every panel is labeled;
copy collapses cleanly to a two-sided one-sheet if we go that route instead.

**Distribution context:** Matthew hands these out in person at shops. The brochure is the
leave-behind for a ~30-second human interaction — it must survive being read hours later,
cold, by someone who forgot the conversation.

## INSIDE PANEL 1 — ROs AND INVOICES, NOT SCRAP PAPER

*Label:* `§ 02 / QUOTE IT ONCE. NEVER LOSE IT.`

Say what you found out loud — "front pads and rotors, left caliper's seized, call it
two and a half hours" — and Lift turns it into line items at your labor rate. Scan the
VIN and the vehicle fills itself in. Snap photos straight onto the RO.

Then it drafts the customer text in plain English. **You read it, change anything you
want, and hit send. Nothing goes to a customer you didn't approve.** They tap once to
approve the estimate, and the RO moves itself to "in repair."

No phone tag. No lost legal pad. No "what did you say it'd run me?"

---

## INSIDE PANEL 2 — THE TEXTS HANDLE THEMSELVES

*Label:* `§ 03 / WHILE YOU WERE UNDER A HOOD, LIFT ANSWERED 4 TEXTS`

A customer texts your shop number: "is my car ready?"
Lift reads it, checks the RO, and texts back the real status — in under 10 seconds.
You never touched your phone.

It only auto-answers status checks. A real question, a complaint, anything that needs
you — that comes straight to you, untouched. Every auto-reply is tagged so you can read
exactly what it said, and there's a kill switch if you ever want it off.

*Visual slot: SMS thread mockup — inbound "is it ready?" + tagged auto-reply.*

---

## INSIDE PANEL 3 — PAID BEFORE PICKUP

*Label:* `§ 04 / STOP CHASING MONEY AFTER THE CAR'S GONE`

When she's ready, the customer gets a text with a pay link. They pay from their phone
before they show up. Keys change hands, done — no wallet fumbling at the counter, no
awkward 90 seconds, no chasing checks.

Your whole day lives on one board: today's cars, grouped by status, on your phone.
No back-office computer. No Sunday-night data entry.

## Copy rules honored (check on every revision)

- [ ] Front panel works with zero other panels read.
- [ ] Specific numbers: $79, 10 seconds, 10 minutes, 14 days, 1–3 bays, three screens.
- [ ] "You approve every word" appears before any customer-facing AI claim sinks in.
- [ ] Anti-persona stated plainly (trust move).
- [ ] No fabricated testimonials, stats, or logos (pre-launch — we have none).
- [ ] No "platform / solution / seamless / powerful / revolutionize."
- [ ] Data-export promise present (lock-in fear).
- [ ] Founder is named, human, and reachable.
